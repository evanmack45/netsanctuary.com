Can I ask a company to show me, or give me a copy of, the data it holds on me under the Texas privacy law?

Yes, if the company is a covered controller and you are a Texas resident.

Full short answer

Yes, if the company is a covered controller and you are a Texas resident. Section 541.051(b)(1) gives you the right to confirm whether it is processing your personal data and to access it. Section 541.051(b)(4) adds a portable copy of the data you previously provided, if it is available in digital format.

Read it in: Texas Data Privacy and Security Act.

The two statute items differ in reach. The copy right in (b)(4) is limited to data you previously provided to the company, and only if it is available in digital format. Item (b)(1) does not carry that limit in its text. The statute does not say what form access under (b)(1) must take.
