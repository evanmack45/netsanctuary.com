Can I sue a company that ignored my Texas privacy request?

Short answer

Not under this law.

Full short answer

Not under this law. Section 541.156 says the chapter cannot be construed as providing a basis for a private right of action, and section 541.151 gives the Attorney General exclusive authority to enforce it. Before suing, the Attorney General must give written notice and 30 days to cure.

Checked against the sources below, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.
