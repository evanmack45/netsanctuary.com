Can a Texas parent ask data brokers to delete a child's data?

Short answer

Yes, for a known child under 13.

Full short answer

Yes, for a known child under 13. Section 541.051(a) lets a parent or legal guardian exercise the child's rights. The statute does not give the same wording for teenagers. The letter builder in this pack is written for an adult asking about their own data and has no parent version.

Checked against the sources below, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

What the statute says

Read it in: Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act).

The definitions that matter:

"Child" means an individual younger than 13 years of age.

From Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), Texas Constitution and Statutes.
