My listing came back after I opted out. What should I check?

Short answer

Spokeo says new public records can bring a listing back without notice, and that you can have several listings, each opted out one at a time. An opt-out does not touch the source record. Check for new listings on a schedule you set and log each one.

Checked against the sources below on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we are not your agent.

What Spokeo says

Opting out your listing from Spokeo will not remove the data from its original source. Your information may still appear on other websites.

Furthermore, you may have multiple listings on Spokeo. Each one is identified by a unique URL and must be opted out individually.

Since we continually receive new and updated records from public sources, your information may reappear on Spokeo in the future without notice. Please regularly check Spokeo for additional listings that may appear.

From Opt out your listing from Spokeo, Spokeo.

Spokeo's help page on addresses adds a limit:

We are not able to remove the entire listing. However, we respect your privacy preferences and you can easily remove individual names associated with the address.

From How do I remove my address from search results?, Spokeo Help Center.

How often you can ask again

Chapter 541 lets a company limit repeat requests:

If a request from a consumer is manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive, the controller may charge the consumer a reasonable fee to cover the administrative costs of complying with the request or may decline to act on the request.

The controller bears the burden of demonstrating for purposes of this subsection that a request is manifestly unfounded, excessive, or repetitive.

From Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), Texas Constitution and Statutes.

A new listing is a new fact, so a new request about it is not the same request twice. The law does not define "repetitive" here.

A routine for it

The free pack includes a tracker file with one row per company. These steps use it:

  • Write the company, the page where you sent the request, and the date.
  • Write the listing's web address in the notes column, because Spokeo says each listing has its own.
  • Pick a date to search again. Spokeo says to check regularly and gives no interval, so the date is yours to choose.
  • When a listing returns, add a new row with a new date instead of overwriting the old one.

What these pages do not say

  • How often listings come back. Spokeo says "regularly" and gives no number.
  • Whether other sites behave the way Spokeo describes. We read only Spokeo's pages for this question.
