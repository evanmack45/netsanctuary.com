Is there one Texas opt-out form for data brokers? What DROP Texas does and what this pack does

Short answer

DROP Texas is one free form

Full short answer

DROP Texas is one free form, run by a private company, that sends opt-out-of-sale requests to the companies it names, nine of them on its home page. This pack sends nothing. It writes deletion letters you send yourself, to any company. For the companies DROP Texas names, it is less work.

Checked against the sources below, 25 Nov 2026. Not legal advice.

What DROP Texas says it is

Read it in: Data brokers: what they hold and how Texans opt out.
