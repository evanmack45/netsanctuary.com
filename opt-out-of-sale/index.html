How do I opt out of the sale of my data, targeted ads, or profiling under the Texas privacy law?

Short answer

You send a covered company a request under section 541.051(b)(5) to opt out of one or more of three things: targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or profiling in furtherance of a decision with a legal or similarly significant effect.

The three opt-outs

Section 541.051(b)(5) gives the right to:

opt out of the processing of the personal data for purposes of:

From Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), Texas Constitution and Statutes.

followed by three items: targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, and profiling in furtherance of a decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect concerning the consumer. Each term has a definition in section 541.001.

What the company must tell you

Read it in: Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act).

That is section 541.103. The privacy notice under section 541.102 also has to describe how to submit requests. Look for both on the company's website before you write your own request.
