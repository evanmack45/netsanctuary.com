A people-search site refused my request. Why does "publicly available" matter?

Both Texas laws leave out publicly available information, which includes government records.

Full short answer

Both Texas laws leave out publicly available information, which includes government records. People-search sites say they build listings from public sources, so they can refuse a letter. A site's own opt-out page is a second route, and a refusal must come with a reason and appeal steps.
