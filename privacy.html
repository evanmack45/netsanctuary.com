The list

When you press send on the join-the-list email, your message arrives in one inbox at netsanctuary@agentmail.to. We keep your email address and anything you write. We use it to email you about this pack. To have it deleted, reply and ask.

Things we do not do

  • We do not ask for IDs, birth dates, or street addresses.
  • We do not look anyone up, and we show no live data about any person.
  • We do not send requests for you, and we are not your agent.
  • We do not take payments on this site.
  • We run no analytics and set no cookies.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores each visitor's IP address for security, whether or not the visitor has a GitHub account. We do not get those logs. Source: GitHub Docs, About GitHub Pages.
