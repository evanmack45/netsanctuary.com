How do I remove my home address from data broker sites? The steps two sites publish

Short answer

Both sites ask you to find your own listing, copy its web address, and paste it into their form. Spokeo asks for an email address and sends a confirmation link. Whitepages describes an automated tool that asks for a phone number, with an email alternative. Spokeo says its opt-out does not remove the record from its original source.

Checked against the sources below on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, America/Chicago. This is a reading guide, not legal advice, and we are not your agent.

Spokeo

Spokeo's help page lists the steps. It says what an address removal can and cannot do:

We are not able to remove the entire listing. However, we respect your privacy preferences and you can easily remove individual names associated with the address.

From How do I remove my address from search results?, Spokeo Help Center.

The steps, in Spokeo's order:

  • Open an incognito window in your browser.
  • Find the address listing and the name you want removed.
  • Right-click the report name and choose Copy link address.
  • Open Spokeo's opt-out page and paste the address into the box.
  • Enter your email address. Spokeo says it uses it to verify the removal.
  • Check the captcha box.

Spokeo's opt-out page adds that it sends a confirmation email and that you must click the link, and it gives a time:

Depending on the nature of your request and the amount of data, your opt-out request should be processed in 24-48 hours.

To complete this process, we will send you a confirmation email. Please click the link in the email.

From Opt out your listing from Spokeo, Spokeo.

Whitepages

Whitepages' opt-out tool opens with this line:

You can opt out and have your listing removed from the Whitepages website. Copy and paste the URL of your profile.

From Opt-out of Whitepages, Whitepages.

Its consumer rights page gives the steps to find the address of your listing:

  • Visit whitepages.com.
  • Enter your name and city and state in the People Search filter.
  • Click the listing that matches you.
  • Copy the web address from your browser.
  • Paste it into the opt-out tool.

In the California section of that page, Whitepages describes the tool's requirements:

You will need to provide a phone number and agree to Whitepages' Terms of Service. The phone number will not be used for any purpose other than completing your opt-out request.

If you would prefer not to provide a phone number or agree to Whitepages' Terms of Service, please submit a request to a customer service agent using the first or second option (online form or email) listed above.

From Consumer Privacy Rights, Whitepages.

That text sits under the California heading. The Texas heading points to a separate FAQ for removal. Read the current Whitepages page before you enter anything.

Limits both sites state

Opting out your listing from Spokeo will not remove the data from its original source. Your information may still appear on other websites.

From Opt out your listing from Spokeo, Spokeo.

The listing-came-back page covers what to log when a listing returns.

What these pages do not say

  • Whether removal is permanent. Spokeo says listings can reappear.
  • Steps for any site other than these two. We did not read other sites' pages, and several block automated reads.
