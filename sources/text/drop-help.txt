Privacy help for Texans who use the DROP Texas form Privacy help What is DROP Texas? DROP Texas is a free service with a form you use to ask data brokers not to sell your personal information. You fill out one form and send the requests. Brokers decide whether they have a record and how they respond. What does a request cost? The service does not charge a fee. You can also opt out yourself at each broker, for free. There is no subscription for making the request through this site. What do I enter? The form asks for your name (including an optional middle name), date of birth, mailing address, email, Texas residency, and, if you choose to provide it, your phone number. For a child request, a parent or guardian also provides the child's name, date of birth and, if different, address. The page needs JavaScript to show the form. It does not ask for a Social Security number, a password, or a device ID. What do the statuses mean? If a status is shown later, it is one of Submitted, Confirmed, Pending, Not completed, or No record found. None of those words means a deletion was verified. DROP Texas does not check that a broker deleted a record. What if a broker does not respond? You send the request. A company still decides how to answer. If a status stays Pending or Not completed, that is what is known. The statute's appeal and attorney general complaint path is described on the TDPSA page. This site is not a government complaint form. Is this a government site? DROP Texas is an independent, free service run by a private enterprise. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with the State of Texas or the Texas Attorney General.
