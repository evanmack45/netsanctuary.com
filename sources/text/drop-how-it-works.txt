Opt out of data brokers using the free DROP Texas form Opt out of data brokers You opt out of data brokers from this form. You can stop at any time before you submit. After you submit, you have sent the requests. Brokers answer on their own schedule. No government agency reviews this form. The form asks for your name (including an optional middle name), date of birth, mailing address, email, Texas residency, and (if you choose to provide it) your phone number. Most broker opt-out forms require a physical address. The form does not ask for a device ID, a password, or a government ID number. Your information is stored securely and auto-deleted per our retention policy After a live submit, your opt-out requests go to the companies on the brokers page. You do not fill out a separate form at each company.
