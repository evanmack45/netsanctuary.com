Does the Texas privacy law make companies honor Global Privacy Control and browser opt-out signals?

Section 541.055 lets a consumer use an Internet browser setting or extension

Full short answer

Section 541.055 lets a consumer use an Internet browser setting or extension, or a global device setting, to opt out of targeted advertising and the sale of personal data. A company must comply if it can verify your identity and the agent's authority. The signal must be your own choice, not a default.

So a browser that turns the signal on for everyone by default does not meet the rule. You have to choose it.

What a signal covers

Subsection (e) points to section 541.051(b)(5)(A) and (B). Those are the rights to opt out of targeted advertising and of the sale of personal data. A signal does not ask a company to delete anything. For deletion, see the 45-day page.
