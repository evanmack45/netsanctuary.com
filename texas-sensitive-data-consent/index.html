What counts as sensitive data under the Texas privacy law, and when is my consent required?

Short answer

Section 541.001 lists sensitive data: data revealing race or ethnicity, religion, a health diagnosis, sexuality, or citizenship or immigration status; genetic or biometric data used to identify you; data from a known child; and precise geolocation. A company covered by the Act may not process it without your consent. A small business may not sell it without your prior consent.

Small businesses and sales

A small business is outside most of the Act, with one exception. Section 541.107 says:

A person described by Section 541.002(a)(3) may not engage in the sale of personal data that is sensitive data without receiving prior consent from the consumer.

From Texas Business & Commerce Code chapter 541 (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act), Texas Constitution and Statutes.

Using this with a request

The Act lets you ask a company to confirm whether it processes your personal data and to give you access to it. The free pack letter has a box for that request. The statute does not say a company must tell you which of your data it treats as sensitive.
